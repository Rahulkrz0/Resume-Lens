# Build Resume + AI Resume Analyzer

Create professional resumes with multiple templates and color themes. Users can build a resume, customize its design, and get AI-powered feedback to improve their chances of getting hired.

## Features

### 1. Resume Builder

* Create resumes easily with simple forms
* Add personal details, education, skills, projects, experience, and achievements
* Live preview while editing

### 2. Multiple Templates & Colors

* Choose from different modern resume templates
* Select different color themes
* Download resumes as PDF
* Clean and ATS-friendly designs

### 3. Resume Upload & Analysis

* Upload an existing resume in PDF format
* Automatically extract resume content for analysis

### 4. Role-Based Evaluation

Choose a target role:

* Backend Developer
* Frontend Developer
* HR / Human Resources
* Other (custom role)

If no role is selected, AI evaluates the resume using general best practices.

### 5. AI Resume Analysis

AI provides:

* Resume score (out of 100)
* Strengths of the resume
* Missing skills and sections
* Content and keyword suggestions
* ATS-friendly feedback
* Actionable improvement tips

### 6. Processing Experience

Show loading messages while AI is working:

* "Analyzing your resume..."
* "Scoring your resume..."
* "Generating detailed feedback..."

After completion:

* Display the resume score instantly
* Show success confirmation after email delivery

### 7. Email Report Automation

Generate a professional resume analysis report and send it automatically via Gmail.

The email includes:

* Resume score
* Role-based feedback
* Strengths and weaknesses
* Missing skills
* Improvement suggestions
* Final recommendation

Display message:
📩 Your detailed resume report has been sent to your email successfully.

## Tech Stack

* Replit (Full Stack)
* React + Tailwind CSS
* PDF Upload & Extraction
* OpenAI or Gemini API
* Gmail Integration
* Multiple Resume Templates & Themes

## Target Users

* Students
* Freshers
* Job Seekers
* Developers preparing for interviews

## Goal

Help users create beautiful resumes, customize them with different templates and colors, analyze their resumes using AI, instantly see their score, and receive a detailed improvement report through email.
